A boutique wedding and event studio, based in Sydney.

Approach

We work with a small number of couples each season. Coverage is led by Julian, with a second photographer on the larger packages. Communications run through email and Instagram, with a one-business-day reply window.

As featured

Our work has been published in Wedded Wonderland, GQ Australia, VOGUE, Harpers Bazaar AU, Bode Magazine, WHO Magazine, Modern Wedding, The Wed and The Lane.
